## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes digital transaction trends, user adoption, and device usage across Indian states, with a focus on **Uttar Pradesh**.

The goal is to uncover meaningful patterns in:
- Transaction growth  
- User behavior  
- Regional adoption  
- Device preferences  

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze transaction trends over time  
- Understand transaction type distribution  
- Evaluate user-to-population ratio  
- Study device brand usage patterns  
- Correlate demographic data with transaction activity  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 📈 Transaction Growth
- Transactions increased consistently from 2018 to 2021  
- Sharp growth observed after 2020  
- Transaction amount rises faster than transaction count  

---

### 🧾 Transaction Distribution
- Recharge & bill payments dominate (~42%)  
- Peer-to-peer transactions are nearly equal (~39%)  
- Merchant payments contribute ~18%  
- Other categories are minimal  

---

### 📊 User-Population Ratio
- **Delhi** shows highest digital adoption  
- Followed by **Dadra & Nagar Haveli**, **Telangana**  
- Indicates strong urban digital penetration  

---

### 📱 Device Usage Insights
- Different states show different brand preferences  
- Budget smartphone brands dominate in high population states  
- Device availability influences digital payment adoption  

---

### 🔗 Correlation Analysis
- Weak to moderate relationship between population density and transactions  
- High density ≠ always high transactions  
- Other factors like literacy & infrastructure matter  

---

## 💡 Conclusion

India’s digital payment ecosystem is growing rapidly.  
While population density has some influence, **technology access, smartphone usage, and digital awareness** are the real drivers of adoption.
